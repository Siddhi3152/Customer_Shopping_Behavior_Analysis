# Customer_Shopping_Behavior_Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 customer purchases** across different product categories.

The objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, product preferences and subscription behavior, and convert the findings into **actionable business insights and recommendations**.

The project follows a business-focused analysis approach involving **data preparation, SQL analysis, exploratory analysis and Power BI visualization**.

## Business Problem
A retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve:
* Sales and revenue
* Customer engagement
* Customer satisfaction
* Customer loyalty
* Marketing and product strategies

The analysis focuses on understanding how factors such as **discounts, product categories, customer demographics, reviews, subscriptions and shipping preferences** influence customer behavior.

## Key Business Questions
The analysis was performed to answer questions such as:

* Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
* How does spending differ between male and female customers?
* Do subscribers spend more than non-subscribers?
* Which products and categories perform best?
* Which products are most dependent on discounts?
* Are repeat customers more likely to subscribe?
* Which age groups generate the highest revenue?
* How do different shipping options compare in terms of purchase value?

## Tools & Technologies
* **Python** – Data cleaning, preparation and exploratory analysis
* **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
* **PostgreSQL / SQL** – Business transaction analysis
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization

## Project Approach

### 1. Data Preparation
* Loaded and explored the customer transaction dataset using Python.
* Checked data structure and missing values.
* Handled missing review ratings using category-level median values.
* Standardized column names.
* Created additional features such as customer age groups and purchase frequency.

### 2. SQL Analysis
The cleaned data was loaded into PostgreSQL for structured business analysis.
Key analyses included:
* Revenue by gender
* High-spending discount users
* Top-rated products
* Shipping type comparison
* Subscriber vs. non-subscriber spending
* Discount-dependent products
* Customer segmentation
* Top products by category
* Repeat buyers and subscription behavior
* Revenue by age group

### 3. Power BI Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to present key findings in an easy-to-understand visual format.
The dashboard includes:
* Customer and purchase KPIs
* Revenue by product category
* Sales by category
* Subscription status
* Revenue by age group
* Sales by age group
* Customer and product filters

## Key Business Insights
The analysis was used to identify opportunities related to:
* Increasing customer subscriptions
* Strengthening customer loyalty
* Reviewing discount strategies
* Promoting high-performing products
* Targeting high-revenue customer segments

## Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:
1. **Boost Subscriptions** – Promote exclusive benefits to encourage customers to subscribe.
2. **Strengthen Loyalty Programs** – Reward repeat customers and encourage them to move into the loyal customer segment.
3. **Review Discount Strategy** – Evaluate discount usage while maintaining healthy margins.
4. **Improve Product Positioning** – Highlight highly rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.
5. **Targeted Marketing** – Focus campaigns on high-revenue customer segments and relevant purchasing behaviors.
